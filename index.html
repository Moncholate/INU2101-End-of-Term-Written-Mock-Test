A trip to remember

My name is Lucas and last summer I went on an amazing trip to Europe. I went with my friend Marco, but our families did not come with us. We traveled for two weeks. It was the first time we were away from home alone.


We went to three countries: Poland, the Czech Republic, and Hungary.


We started in Warsaw, the capital city of Poland. Marco loved the city, but I did not like it very much. The streets were very cold and it rained every day.


Then we traveled to Prague, in the Czech Republic. It was my favorite city on the trip. We walked a lot and saw beautiful old buildings. We also had fantastic food there. One day, we did not have a map and we walked for three hours in the wrong direction. We talked a lot about it after.


After Prague, we traveled to Budapest, in Hungary. We walked next to the Danube River and saw the famous Chain Bridge. At night, the city looked beautiful with all the lights. We stayed in a small hostel near the center. The people there were very friendly and helped us a lot.


After two weeks, we went back home. We were very tired, but very happy. It was a trip to remember.

1. How long was the trip? 1 pt
2. Who did NOT enjoy the first city? 1 pt
3. What did they do in Prague? Write 2 ideas. 2 pts
4. What did the narrator like about Budapest? Write 2 ideas. 2 pts
